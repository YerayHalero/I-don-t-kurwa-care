Kurwa

[ˈkur.va]wykrzyknik · interj.stress on first syllable, rolled r
  1. Punctuation.No i kurwa, poszło.And just like that, it worked.
  2. Determination.Kurwa, damy radę.We'll get it done. No discussion.
  3. Admiration.O kurwa, ale robota.Said by the foreman when the job is perfect.
  4. Attitude.I don't kurwa care.Say what you want. We'll keep building.
We built half of Europe. We don't need permission to be proud of it.

IDKC is for the ones who left Poland and took it with them. The early shift in London, the site in Oslo, the hotel night desk in Copenhagen. Work hard, say it straight, wear red and white like you mean it.

And for everyone who worked next to a Pole and came home with a new favourite word.
